Peanut Butter Apple Nachos

Ingredients

  • 1 Apple, sliced thin
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • Granola or crushed nuts
  • Chocolate drizzle(optional)

Instructions

  1. Layer apple slices on a plate.
  2. Drizzle with melted peanut butter.
  3. Add the chocolate drizzle as well and sprickle the granola or crushed nuts
