Mini Caprese Skewers

Ingredients

  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Mini mozzarella balls
  • Fresh Basil
  • Balsamic glaze

Instructions

  1. Skewer one tomato, one mozarella ball, and a basil leaf onto tootpicks.
  2. Drizzle with Balsamic glaze just before serving.
