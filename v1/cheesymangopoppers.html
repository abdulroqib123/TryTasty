Cheesy Mango Poppers

Ingredients

  • Ripe mango cubes
  • Cream cheese
  • Crushed nachos or cornflakes
  • Toothpicks

Instructions

  1. Freeze mango cubes for 30 minutes.
  2. Wrap each cube with a thin layer of cream cheese.
  3. Roll in crushed nachos or cornflakes.
  4. Serve chilled with toothpicks.
