sweet Potato Bites With Yogurt Dip

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Cinnamon + paprika/tomatoe
  • Salt
  • Greek yogurt(or any available yogurt you can get) + lemon juice for dip

Instructions

  1. Cut sweet potatoes into small cubes, toss with oil, cinamon, paprika, and salt.
  2. Bake at 200°C(400°F) for 25 mins.
  3. Mix yogurt with a splash of lemon juice.
  4. Serve as the yogurt as dip with the baked potatoes.
